DregoraRL SRP Manhunt 3.10.11 — faction clear-zone matrix

CORE RULE
A structure's valuable loot follows the faction that actually has to fight there.
Friendly territory is not a profitable free-loot route. Low-value ambient junk may stay universal,
but progression loot, boss rewards and high-tier caches follow the raid side.

1) VAGRANT RAID ZONES — HUMAN/MILITARY DEFENDERS
Structures:
- Military Bunkers (both bunker structure sets)
- Nuclear Power Plant
- Radio Telescope

Enemies:
- MCHeli foot gunners / generated garrisons
- Turret Mod defenses where markers exist
- Guard tanks / military vehicles / helicopters where markers exist
- Vanilla fallback guards only when optional military integrations cannot spawn

Loot:
- manhunt_vagrant_scrap: low-value Cotesia salvage
- manhunt_military_common: Vagrant common Cotesia salvage
- manhunt_military_rare: Vagrant rare Cotesia salvage
- manhunt_strategic_cache: Vagrant strategic Cotesia cache
- Human firearms/police-armory/industrial/generic high-value storage is removed from defended military sites.

Boss:
- Radio Telescope = MCHeli boss helicopter.
- Boss/clear reward side = VAGRANT only.

2) HUMAN RAID ZONES — PARASITE DEFENDERS
Structures:
- Infected megacity skyscrapers / parasite destruction-layer areas

Enemies:
- Existing SRParasites spawners remain the primary threat.
- Principal 8x6 infected skyscraper contains one guaranteed Human-side parasite boss marker.

Loot:
- manhunt_infected_cache: human field supplies + compatible ammunition
- manhunt_laboratory_common / laboratory_rare: human progression supplies + ammunition
- manhunt_human_strategic_cache: human weapons/armor/ammunition strategic reward
- Military/strategic Vagrant caches are removed from infected skyscrapers.
- Ambient residential/junk/toilet/city-potion rolls may remain low-value universal scavenging; they are not the progression reward.

Boss:
- One of: Crux, Bogle, Flying Carrier, Overseer, Adapted Yelloweye.
- Spawned once from the consumed encounter marker, not from an endlessly reusable boss spawner.
- Boss/clear reward side = HUMAN only.

3) NEUTRAL / COMMON RAID ZONES — ENEMIES OF BOTH FACTIONS
Structures:
- Dungeon 01
- Dungeon 02
- Dungeon 03
- Brutal Tower

Enemies:
- Ordinary encounter markers/spawners use tagged vanilla hostile mobs.
- Tagged neutral mobs actively reacquire the nearest player regardless of Human/Vagrant side.
- Dungeon bosses are vanilla Wither Skeleton / Elder Guardian variants and count as neutral reward bosses.

Loot:
- neutral_common / neutral_rare / neutral_epic = vanilla resources usable by either faction.
- No Human-only gun progression and no Vagrant-only Cotesia progression is required to make the zone worthwhile.

Brutal Tower boss and completion:
- Final boss = persistent Wither at the top-floor boss position.
- Full-clear volume = 128 blocks horizontal / 192 blocks vertical.
- Jackpot appears only after the Wither is killed and all tagged neutral tower mobs in the volume are gone.
- Jackpot: guaranteed diamonds, emeralds and XP plus high-value vanilla rolls (Totem / Nether Star / Golden Apple / rare Elytra) and bulk resources.

4) ORDINARY WORLD / LOW-VALUE COMMON SCAVENGING
Structures not explicitly promoted to a faction raid remain normal exploration content.
Their low-value generic loot does not grant a faction completion reward and is intentionally not treated as a replacement for military/infected raid progression.

BOSS REWARD RULES
- Human boss: direct killer must be non-Vagrant; remaining nearby parasites/hostile vanilla mobs block completion.
- Vagrant boss: Vagrant eligibility is required; remaining generated defenders/hostile vanilla mobs block completion.
- Neutral boss: either faction is eligible; remaining tagged neutral mobs block completion.
- Existing persistent clear-marker/cooldown prevents repeated reward farming in the same cleared area.

SPAWNER CONTROL
- DregoraRL's 6-gunner / 48-block runtime area cap is removed.
- The associated two config options are removed.
- Reworked neutral spawners and retained garrison spawner payloads use MaxNearbyEntities=32767.
- Population limiting is delegated to the modpack's dedicated spawner-control mod.

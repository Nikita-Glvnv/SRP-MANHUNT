DregoraRL: SRP Manhunt Edition 3.10.11
Faction zones, neutral dungeons and Brutal Tower overhaul

ZONE MODEL
- Military territory (Bunkers, Radio Telescope, Nuclear Power Plant) is Vagrant PvE: generated gunners, turrets and MCHeli vehicles remain the defenders. Useful military storage now resolves to SRPCotesia/Cotesia salvage instead of human firearms and generic high-tier loot.
- Radio Telescope is the Vagrant military boss-site: its former guard-helicopter marker is upgraded to the existing high-HP MCHeli boss helicopter. Destroying it can arm only a Vagrant-side clear reward, and the reward still waits for the defended site to be cleared.
- Infected megacity territory is Human PvE: parasite spawners remain the threat. Military/strategic skyscraper caches are remapped to laboratory/human strategic tables, and infected caches no longer contain parasite materials that a Vagrant could farm from allied territory.
- The principal infected 8x6 skyscraper now has one guaranteed Human-side parasite boss encounter. It randomly selects Crux, Bogle, Flying Carrier, Overseer or Adapted Yelloweye, marks it as a Human reward boss, and uses the existing full-clear logic so a Vagrant cannot claim the Human completion reward.
- Dungeon 01-03 are neutral PvE: their defense markers and ordinary spawners now use tagged vanilla hostile mobs. Existing dungeon bosses are neutral reward bosses, so either faction can complete them.
- Brutal Tower is a fully neutral endgame PvE structure. Every generated turret/gunner/aircraft marker in the tower is replaced by vanilla-hostile squads, and every tower mob-spawner payload referenced by the BO4s is replaced by vanilla enemies.

BRUTAL TOWER
- The central top-floor boss slot at Y=228 is now a persistent Wither boss. It is tagged as the neutral structure boss and can reward either faction.
- The old SRParasites/custom Brutal Tower spawner payloads are no longer used by the tower; their referenced NBT files now spawn only Minecraft hostiles such as zombies, skeletons, spiders, cave spiders, husks, strays, witches, blazes, vexes and wither skeletons.
- Tier 1-2 crates use neutral_common, tier 3-4 neutral_rare, tier 5-6 and treasure use neutral_epic. These tables contain broadly usable vanilla resources rather than faction-specific items.
- Killing the final Wither arms a full-tower clear check (128 blocks horizontal / 192 vertical). The jackpot is awarded only after all tagged neutral tower mobs in that volume are gone.
- Final jackpot is intentionally very rich: guaranteed 8-16 diamonds, 12-24 emeralds, 16-32 XP bottles, two high-value rolls (golden apple / totem / nether star / rare elytra) and 3-5 large vanilla-resource rolls.

SPAWNER CONTROL
- Removed DregoraRL's runtime area cap that cancelled MCHeli foot-gunner spawns at 6 entities / 48 blocks.
- Removed the two related config options entirely.
- Neutral dungeon/tower spawners and the retained Nuclear Power Plant gunner spawner use MaxNearbyEntities=32767, effectively removing this preset's local population limiter and leaving population control to the modpack's dedicated spawner-control mod.
- Activation ranges and ordinary spawn delays remain so spawners still behave like actual Minecraft spawners rather than permanent always-loaded entity emitters.

ANTI-FARM / CLEAR REWARDS
- Human, Vagrant and neutral completion rewards remain kill-gated and full-clear-gated.
- Neutral reward eligibility accepts either faction. Human and Vagrant rewards remain faction-specific.
- The existing persistent anti-farm marker/cooldown remains in place.

GENERATION SAFETY
- Structure roots, branches, coordinates, biome graph and world-generation distances are retained.
- Changes are limited to encounter-marker metadata, selected chest-table mappings, selected spawner NBT payloads and runtime faction/reward logic.

DregoraRL SRP Manhunt 3.10.66
OTG biome/stronghold startup hotfix

The 3.10.65 clean-world crash was caused by four RiverBiome settings pointing
to removed configs. OTG's standard river layer emitted biome ID -1, and the
single-threaded stronghold biome search dereferenced that missing biome during
spawn preparation.

Fixed mappings:
- Military Frontier -> River
- Urban Canal -> River
- Wasteland -> DesertRiver
- Ruins of Blight Border -> Nuclear Creek

The arena now generates exactly one stronghold:
- StrongholdCount: 1
- StrongholdDistance: 32.0
- StrongholdSpread: 1

Malformed ReplacedBlocks syntax in Nuclear Creek and Urban Canal and the stale
Stone Beach "Jungle Edge" reference were also corrected. CityGrid, metro,
roads, structures, loot and encounters are unchanged from 3.10.65.

A completely new OTG world is required.

DregoraRL SRP Manhunt 3.10.70 — CITY / ROADS / OUTSKIRTS RUNTIME RESTORE

ROOT CAUSE
- WorldConfig used BiomeMode: Normal although the project was authored as a
  bounded FromImage sector preset.
- The bundled preset did not contain map.png.
- Only Coast, DesertCoast and DesertRiver were registered in CustomBiomes;
  Ruins of Blight and the other 15 Manhunt biomes were therefore unavailable.
- CityGrid is selected only by Ruins of Blight, so OTG never reached the city
  root, its roads, metro, highways or outskirts branches.
- SpawnPointSet=true and Java code then forced the player toward (0,0), hiding
  the western city sector even if a stale installation still contained a map.

FIX
- Restored a 2504x2504 exact-colour indexed map.png for the 10K world.
- Restored FromImage / North / -1252,-1252 configuration.
- Registered all Manhunt virtual biomes at OTG generation IDs 256-271.
- Made all BiomeColor values unique for FromImage lookup.
- Restored OTG ownership of spawn and removed the hard-coded zero-coordinate
  city assumption.
- CityGrid Frequency=1 plus manhunt_city:313 guarantees one root inside the
  single Ruins of Blight region without recurring cities.

EXPECTED LOCATION
- The infected city sector is in the west, centred approximately around
  X=-2960, Z=-192.
- On a completely new world OTG should set the initial spawn at the CityGrid
  root; first-login safety logic searches road columns relative to that real
  root rather than relative to (0,0).

IMPORTANT
- Existing worlds and existing OTG preset folders retain generated chunks and
  structure caches. Delete the old world and allow version 3.10.70 to replace
  mods/OpenTerrainGenerator/worlds/DregoraRL_SRPManhunt before testing.

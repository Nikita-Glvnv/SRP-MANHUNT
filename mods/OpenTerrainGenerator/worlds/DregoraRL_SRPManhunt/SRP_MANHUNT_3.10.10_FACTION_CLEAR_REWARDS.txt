DregoraRL: SRP Manhunt Edition 3.10.10
Foot-gunner spawners and faction clear rewards

GENERATION SAFETY
- The six-biome post-apocalyptic generation graph is unchanged from 3.10.9.
- No biome, structure root, branch, vanilla-structure switch or structure
  distance was removed or renamed.
- All 417 fixed BO3/BO4 MOB_SPAWNER placements, all 384 RandomBlock spawner
  records and their 489 weighted alternatives remain at previous coordinates.
- The 162 defended-structure spawner references are retained: 78 in Brutal
  Tower, 64 across Dungeons 01-03 and 20 in Nuclear Power Plant.
- Existing encounter markers, vehicles, turrets, bosses, boss health,
  equipment, effects and original boss loot tables remain in place.

SPAWNER FOOT GUNNERS
- The seven formerly dormant non-boss spawner payloads now use the registered
  MCHeli CE entity mcheli:MCH.E.Gunner.
- Every activation requests one ordinary gunner without armor/item/effect NBT
  and without custom Health or Attribute values.
- Brutal Tower spawners use 120-240 second delays; dungeon and nuclear
  spawners use 90-180 second delays. All require a player within 16 blocks.
- The runtime allows at most six spawner-created foot gunners in a configurable
  48-block three-dimensional area. Extra spawn attempts are safely cancelled
  and can retry later.
- Spawner gunners join the existing autonomous patrol/target/fire controller.
  They are forcibly dismounted and have the MCHeli seated/sneaking flag cleared
  both when initialized and continuously during their server update.
- A client-only renderer bridge removes MCHeli's normal "do not render without
  an aircraft" gate for tagged foot gunners. It uses the same upright biped and
  Steve texture while retaining the hideEntity rule for mounted vehicle crews.
- Their projectiles remain non-pickup and expire after three seconds.

VAGRANT REWARDS
- Military common, military rare and strategic caches each make one restrained
  salvage check.
- Brutal Tower's 12 randomized processed-goods chests and 19 extracted-goods
  treasure chests make the same restrained salvage check; the much more common
  tier 1-6 crates are left untouched to prevent quantity inflation.
- The check is 94% empty, 4% one Frayed Wires, 1% one Chip Fragment and 1% one
  Ghastly Bandage.
- High-tier SRPCotesia matter, full chips, boosters and unbound Remnants are
  intentionally excluded.
- Seven retained Dungeon 01-03 Wither Skeleton/Elder Guardian boss payloads are
  tagged for a Vagrant clear reward. Their original HP, equipment, effects,
  SpawnPotentials and loot remain unchanged.
- Destruction of the generated MCHeli structure boss can arm a Vagrant reward
  only while an actual SRPCotesia Vagrant is nearby.
- A tagged living dungeon boss arms the same reward only when its direct killer
  is an actual SRPCotesia Vagrant.
- The reward waits until generated gunners, turrets, vehicles and ordinary
  hostile vanilla defenders in the configured structure radius are gone.
  Other tagged Vagrant-side bosses in that radius must also be dead.
- A completed clear gives 1-2 Frayed Wires and one restrained bonus roll:
  52% empty, 18% Chip Fragment, 14% Ghastly Bandage, 9% Brew Sac and
  7% Sparse Chitin.

HUMAN REWARDS
- Infected caches and common laboratory caches receive one additional common
  ammunition roll.
- Common ammunition favors AK-74/AKM/M4/MP5 magazines; pistol magazines remain
  lower-weight choices. The roll is still 28-weight empty.
- Rare laboratory caches receive one advanced-ammunition roll containing
  compatible extended/rifle magazines, very rare grenades and a 1-weight
  ammunition box.
- The same 12 randomized Brutal Tower chests receive a common-ammunition roll;
  its 19 rarer treasure chests receive one advanced roll. The 244 ordinary
  tier 1-6 crate placements are not given extra ammunition.
- The five principal SRParasites Brutal Tower boss SpawnData records are tagged
  for clear rewards without changing any original boss statistic or loot:
  Crux, Bogle, Flying Carrier, Overseer and Adapted Yelloweye.
- A reward is armed only when a non-Vagrant player kills the tagged boss. It
  waits until nearby SRParasites and ordinary hostile vanilla mobs are gone.
- A successful clear guarantees one compatible common magazine, then makes one
  extra common-ammunition roll, one restrained advanced roll and a small
  field-supply roll. Weapons are not added to this completion reward.

ANTI-FARM AND PERSISTENCE
- Pending clear checks use an invisible, invulnerable marker that survives
  save/reload and does not alter the OTG structure files.
- The first check occurs after a five-second grace period.
- A completed marker blocks another reward in the same area for 72,000 loaded
  ticks (one real-time hour at 20 TPS) before removing itself.
- Default clear radius is 72 blocks horizontally and up to 48 blocks vertically.

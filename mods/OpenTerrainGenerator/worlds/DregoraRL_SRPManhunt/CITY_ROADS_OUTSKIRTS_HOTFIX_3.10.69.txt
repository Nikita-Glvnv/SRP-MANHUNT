DregoraRL SRP Manhunt 3.10.69
FINITE CITY / ROADS / OUTSKIRTS SPAWN HOTFIX

ROOT CAUSE
- CityGrid became the mandatory IsSpawnPoint BO4 in 3.10.68 but retained its
  legacy SpawnHeight randomY, MinHeight 50 and MaxHeight 50 settings.
- Because CityGrid has OverrideChildSettings true and the finite graph inherits
  its parent Y, the central city, bypass, roads, suburbs and highways were
  plotted around Y=50 instead of the terrain surface.
- CanSpawnOnWater false could cancel the empty root before FC-CityMacro was
  reached.
- The finite-city graph uses required branches. OTG checks villages and
  scattered features within four chunks and can roll a failed required branch
  back through every parent to CityGrid.

FIX
- CityGrid: highestBlock, height range 0..255, CanSpawnOnWater true,
  ReplaceWithBiomeBlocks false.
- VillagesEnabled false and RareBuildingsEnabled false prevent unrelated
  vanilla land structures from deleting the mandatory city graph.
- Strongholds, mineshafts, dungeons and ocean monuments are unchanged.
- Outbound highway entry and four service-road entries are optional relative
  to the ring, so distant branch failures no longer erase the city core.
- Preset and mod version bumped to 3.10.69 so the installer replaces 3.10.68.

REQUIRED TEST
- Delete/recreate the OTG world (or use a clean instance) before testing.
- Verify the city core, closed bypass, four gates, suburban/service parcels and
  outbound roads from newly generated spawn chunks.

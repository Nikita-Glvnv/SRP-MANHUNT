DregoraRL SRP Manhunt 3.10.65
FINITE CITY / BYPASS / OUTSKIRTS GENERATION

CITY CORE
- The authored L4-M-Cross, buildings, destruction and parking objects are preserved.
- The fixed progression remains Cross -> Start -> arterial/collector -> 3C -> 2C.
- All four arms now terminate in FC-CityEdge instead of returning recursively
  to L4 2C or starting an unbounded L3 graph.
- Opposite city arms retain the previous 8-10-sector progression target.

PERIMETER
- FC-CityMacro correlates the randomly rotated city with one closed bypass.
- The bypass rectangle is 528 x 784 blocks around the authored city centre.
- Its four deliberate gaps are filled by the rotated four-chunk Gate R1
  intersections, avoiding overlapping BO4 pieces.
- Eight optional four-chunk service roads lead to small industrial/residential
  parcels and authored rubble, T-junction, blockpost or bridge endings.

OUTBOUND HIGHWAYS
- Exactly four primary highways leave the four original city arms.
- Each starts only after the bypass and contains no metro or city recursion.
- Seed-randomized authored lengths: 1024, 1536, 2048 or 2496 blocks.
- Long continuations become progressively rarer (80%, 70%, then 45%).
- Roadside progression: private houses -> warehouses -> farms/villages ->
  sparse checkpoints and fields.

BIOME BOUNDARIES
- Ruins of Blight remains the only biome allowed to start CityGrid (100%).
- Every other overworld biome permits CityGrid branches at 0% start chance,
  so a required bypass/highway branch can cross a biome boundary without
  rolling back the city or creating a second city root.
- Hell, Sky and The Void do not receive this overworld permission.

METRO / UNDERGROUND
- Existing inner-city lower geometry and the two restored entrances are unchanged.
- The new edge preserves the original lower corridor through the final block.
- Gate copies the compatible lower geometry, removes stationary water locally,
  and seals both central tunnel chunks with a full technical wall.
- No underground blocks are generated by the highway, bypass or roadside lots.

ROAD END SAFETY
- CityRoadEnd.bo3 remains in source for compatibility but is no longer called
  by Tree(...) in Ruins of Blight, its border, or Nuclear Ruins.
- Every new road ending belongs to the actual branch that it terminates.
- Newly authored bypass, highway, lot, service-road and end-cap surfaces use
  vanilla 1.12 blocks. The cloned final city edge preserves its original
  underground materials and existing parked-car marker.

This patch affects new chunks/new worlds. Existing generated chunks are not rewritten.

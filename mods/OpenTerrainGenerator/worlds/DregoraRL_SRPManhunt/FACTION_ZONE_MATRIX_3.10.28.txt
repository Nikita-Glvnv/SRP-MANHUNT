SRP MANHUNT — FACTION ZONE AUDIT 3.10.28
=========================================

1) VAGRANT / MILITARY RAID ZONES
---------------------------------
Primary structures:
- procedural military bunkers (f_bunker_* family)
- Bunker / AdvancedShelter / UndergroundShelter / Piston-BunkerUnderground
- NuclearPowerPlant
- RadioTelescope military encounter

Enemy layer:
- MCHeli foot gunners (marker 13)
- Turret Mod defenses (markers 0/1/10/11)
- MCHeli ground vehicles / tanks / helicopters (2/3/12)
- marker 4 resolves through the MCHeli garrison path; bow-skeleton fallback is disabled

Targeting:
- generated defenders prioritize Vagrant players
- SRParasites and ordinary hostile vanilla mobs remain valid defensive fallback targets
- non-Vagrant human players are never selected as military-defense targets

Bunker sanitation:
- every bunker family now contains/reaches at least one marker 13
- marker 13 creates a persistent 48-block horizontal military-zone anchor
- ambient vanilla hostile mobs and SRParasites are suppressed inside anchored bunker zones
- this prevents biome husks/zombies/etc. from replacing the intended MCHeli defenders

Foot gunner weapon:
- old vanilla arrow surrogate removed
- real MCHeli weapon instances are used through the reflection bridge
- default weapon pool: mg7_62mm,m60_r
- gunners pursue the selected Vagrant and fire real MCHeli bullets/sounds

Biome placement cleanup:
- Nuclear Blight no longer generates bunkers, NuclearPowerPlant or Quarry
- military bunkers/NPP are concentrated in Wasteland / Rocky Plains
- Rocky Plains no longer has ambient SRParasites natural spawn

2) HUMAN / INFECTED RAID ZONES
-------------------------------
Primary zones:
- Nuclear Ruins
- Ruins of Blight
- Nuclear Blight infected industrial region

Enemy layer:
- parasite structure spawners / SRParasites encounters

Nuclear Blight is now kept free of the military bunker/NPP/Quarry encounter roots, so its
natural SRParasites spawn is not mixed with Vagrant military-clear structures.

3) NEUTRAL / COMMON RAID ZONES

4) DELIBERATE CITY-ROAD EXCEPTION
---------------------------------
The infected megacity still contains the previously requested rare city patrol tank marker 6
and parked-car marker 7 on roads. The parasite skyscraper/boss layer remains the HUMAN raid
content, while the rare road tank is a Vagrant-targeting patrol. This is the only intentional
cross-faction military presence retained inside the megacity.

------------------------
- Polluted Ocean was removed from normal BiomeGroup selection

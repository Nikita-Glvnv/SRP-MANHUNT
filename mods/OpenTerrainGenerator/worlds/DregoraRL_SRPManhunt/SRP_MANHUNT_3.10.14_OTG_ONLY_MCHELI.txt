SRP Manhunt 3.10.14 — OTG-only MCHeli integration
=================================================

Rule
----
MCHeliCE is treated as an external stock dependency. No MCHeli Java/resource file is patched,
replaced or bundled by this project. All encounter behaviour lives in Dregora/OTG.

Generated aircraft initialization
---------------------------------
1. OTG creates the normal MCHeli entity and calls setTypeName/changeType.
2. OTG copies AircraftInfo.getTextureName() into the entity TextureName.
3. After MCHeli's first native update, OTG performs one final initialization pass because stock
   MCHeli resets InfinityAmmo/InfinityFuel from global config in onFirstUpdate().
4. Armed generated vehicles receive native CommonStatus bit 3 (InfinityAmmo) and bit 4
   (InfinityFuel), full fuel and gunner status. The aircraft is then removed from the pending set.

No ammo/fuel polling is used.

Generated mounted gunners
-------------------------
- switchTargetCount is initialized to a very large value, disabling stock broad target AABB scans.
- autoCountermeasureCooldown is initialized to a very large value, disabling the stock guided-
  projectile AABB scan for encounter NPCs.
- OTG selects a visible Vagrant from world.playerEntities once per second.
- If no Vagrant/current target exists, a visible parasite/vanilla-hostile fallback is considered
  at most once per three seconds.
- MCHeli remains responsible for native weapon rotation and firing at the assigned target.

Hunter helicopter
-----------------
Only tagged OTG hunter helicopters are tracked. Four times per second OTG performs constant-time
steering toward the mounted gunner's already-selected Vagrant. The flight controller itself does
not scan entities or raytrace visibility. When the gunner loses visibility on its next one-second
target refresh, the target is cleared and the helicopter stops pursuing.

Boss aircraft
-------------
MCHeli aircraft are not LivingEntity and expose no Forge LivingDeathEvent. OTG therefore checks
only its weak set of tagged boss aircraft twice per second. This is not a world scan and replaces
the old source-level destruction hook.

Foot gunners
------------
Stock MCHeli normally despawns an unmounted gunner. OTG initializes despawnCount to a very
negative value once, which keeps generated foot gunners alive without a per-tick reflection write.
Their movement and lightweight projectile attack remain Dregora-side.

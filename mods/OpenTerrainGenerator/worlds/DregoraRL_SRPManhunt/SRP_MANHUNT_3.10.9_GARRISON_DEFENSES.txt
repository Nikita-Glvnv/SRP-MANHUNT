DregoraRL: SRP Manhunt Edition 3.10.9
Autonomous garrisons and defended-structure cleanup

ACTIVE RANDOM BIOMES
- Nuclear Ruins: infected megacity and skyscrapers.
- Ruins of Blight: infected megacity with SRParasites overgrowth.
- Nuclear Blight: bunkers, factories, power infrastructure and nuclear sites.
- Wasteland: ruined buildings, shelters, antennas and industry.
- Rocky Plains: radio telescope, laboratories, protected dungeons and Brutal Tower.
- Polluted Ocean: oil rigs and underwater industrial bases.

GENERATION SAFETY
- The six-biome post-apocalyptic generation graph is unchanged from 3.10.8.
- Every vanilla structure setting, WorldConfig switch and structure distance is
  unchanged from 3.10.8.
- CustomStructure and Branch references still resolve to the retained objects;
  no structure root, branch, biome or vanilla structure was removed.
- All 417 fixed BO3/BO4 MOB_SPAWNER placements and all 384 RandomBlock
  spawner alternatives remain present at their original coordinates.
- All structure-specific loot tables and active chest-NBT mappings are unchanged.

DEFENDED-STRUCTURE MOB CLEANUP
- Protected areas are military bunkers, Radio Telescope, Nuclear Power Plant,
  Dungeons 01-03 and Brutal Tower.
- The 78 ordinary Brutal Tower spawners, 64 ordinary dungeon spawners and
  20 Nuclear Power Plant spawners remain physically present.
- Those 162 non-boss placements now point to dormant ordinary-vanilla spawner
  payloads: SpawnCount is zero, all delays are 32767 ticks, and the inert
  SpawnData fallback is minecraft:pig without equipment, effects, attributes or
  modified health.
- Thirteen plain wither-skeleton Entity(...) encounters in Dungeons 01-02 were
  removed from generation and replaced with supported turret or foot-garrison
  markers.
- Parasite encounter marker kinds 8 and 9 no longer occur in the preset. Their
  runtime handlers are also converted to defenders so an unconsumed legacy
  marker in an already-generated chunk cannot recreate an infected squad.
- Forty protected Dungeon and Brutal Tower boss NBT files remain byte-for-byte
  identical to 3.10.8. Boss spawners, boss Entity(...) records, boss health and
  boss loot are not changed.

AUTONOMOUS MCHeli FOOT GARRISON
- Marker kind 13 creates a configurable squad of actual MCHeli CE 1.5
  MCH_EntityGunner entities without mounting them in a vehicle.
- MCHeli's native gunner is seat-bound and has no navigator. The generated role
  therefore supplies a lightweight server AI for persistence, local
  floor-aware patrol movement, obstacle jumping, pursuit/spacing, target
  visibility, aiming and ranged attacks.
- Foot gunners patrol around their structure marker, move toward distant targets,
  back away at very close range and fire only with direct line of sight.
- Their non-pickup vanilla projectiles are capped at a three-second lifetime so
  missed shots cannot accumulate around a loaded garrison.
- They have no generated armor, held-item NBT, potion effects, attribute
  modifiers or changed maximum health.
- If MCHeli is absent or a gunner cannot spawn, the existing lightweight vanilla
  ranged-guard fallback remains available.

EXPANDED DEFENSE MAP
- 50 encounter markers now exist across the retained structures and city roads.
- 16 marker-kind-13 foot-garrison positions were added or converted.
- Military bunkers have 18 positions covering tanks, four turret types and
  mobile foot garrisons.
- Brutal Tower has eight defense positions: its protected boss helicopter,
  guard helicopter, minigun, laser, shotgun, flamethrower and two foot squads.
- Dungeons 01-03 have 15 defense positions replacing their ordinary hostile

- Radio Telescope retains its tank, helicopter and turret and now resolves its
  guard marker to actual MCHeli foot gunners when MCHeli is present.
- Existing city patrol tanks and rare parked/fueled cars remain unchanged.

UNIFIED DEFENSE TARGET POLICY
- SRPCotesia remains authoritative for Vagrant state through
  ParasitePlayer.isParasite; there is no scoreboard or team-name fallback.
- Generated turrets, all mounted vehicle/helicopter gunners, city patrol gunners,
  vanilla fallback guards and foot garrisons share the same target policy.
- Valid targets are:
  * SRPCotesia Vagrant players.
  * Living entities registered in the srparasites namespace.
  * Ordinary hostile minecraft entities whose class implements IMob.
- Ordinary human players, passive mobs, neutral Endermen/Zombie Pigmen,
  generated defenders and every entity reporting vanilla/modded boss status
  are excluded.
- Elder Guardians and Wither Skeletons are also excluded so the retained
  dungeon and Brutal Tower boss encounters cannot be mistaken for ordinary
  hostile vanilla mobs.
- Turret Mod receives a static registry whitelist for SRParasites and hostile
  vanilla entity IDs, plus a dynamically refreshed Vagrant-player whitelist.
- Generated-defender tags are denied during Turret Mod target checks, checked
  again by the common living-target guard, and protected by a final
  non-approved-damage cancellation layer to prevent garrison friendly fire.
